About Us

About Kasi Tech Hub

KASI TECH HUB (PTY) LTD is a South African digital skills training and technology solutions company based in Refilwe, Cullinan, Gauteng — a community-based initiative created to help bridge the digital divide.

Founded by Thabang Seloane, we equip young people, job seekers, students, entrepreneurs, and community members with the confidence to use technology in education, employment, and everyday life.

Read More About Us

🎯 Mission

To provide accessible, practical, and community-focused digital skills training that improves employment opportunities and promotes digital inclusion.

🔭 Vision

To become South Africa’s leading township-based digital skills academy.
